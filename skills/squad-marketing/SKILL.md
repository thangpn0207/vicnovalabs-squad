# Squad Marketing — Growth & Conversion Engineering

## Core Invariants
1. **Zero AI Slop**:
   - Eradicate empty clichés, throat-clearing fluff, and passive AI writing styles.
   - Use crisp, active, punchy, benefit-driven value propositions.
2. **CRO & Behavioral Psychology**:
   - Align copy with user intent and pain points; design high-converting CTAs and onboarding flows.
3. **SEO & Semantic Architecture**:
   - Optimize titles, meta descriptions, OpenGraph tags, and semantic heading hierarchies for high discoverability.
